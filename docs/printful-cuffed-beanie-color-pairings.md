## Preserve the original embroidery

The source is `03-sourdough-house-name-only-white-large.png`, one layer at **3.22 × 1.75 inches**, centered on the front cuff. This is the name-only logo, without a dragonfly. Preserve the existing file, size, position, Standard color option and Flat Embroidery technique.

For this embroidery product, color matching uses the thread mapping rather than replacing the PNG. After publication, select only Heather Grey in Published > My Store > Edit, select its one variant, then Change print file. Open Color & details, change 1801 White to 1800 Black, then proceed through mockups. The PNG filename still says white; the saved thread mapping determines the embroidery color.

Saved Heather Grey was reopened after reload: 1800 Black persisted, the original single layer remained 3.22 × 1.75 inches, and its position was unchanged. The eight white-thread colors were left untouched.
