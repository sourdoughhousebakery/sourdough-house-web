# Shopify product title cleanup design

## Goal

Make the Shopify catalog easier to scan by removing the repeated “Sourdough House Bakery” prefix. The storefront already provides brand context in its header and visual identity.

## Naming rules

- Do not use the internal abbreviation “SDHB” in customer-facing titles.
- Lead with the saying or visual design when it distinguishes the product.
- End with the familiar garment or product type.
- Use “Bakery Logo” only when needed to distinguish a logo product from a dragonfly or saying design.
- Preserve existing Shopify handles, variants, prices, media, tags, descriptions, inventory, and Printful mappings.
- Keep SEO branding separate from the visible product title if search titles are optimized later.
