## What happens after someone buys

The order and the money take different paths

1. The customer chooses a product, size and color, then checks out in Shopify. They do not need a Printful account.
2. Shopify records the order and payment. Confirmation is sent using the enabled notification settings and the contact details provided.
3. The paid order reaches Printful under the import settings chosen at handoff. Production may require your approval, or start automatically.
4. Printful charges the bakery for making and shipping the item, then produces it. Typical production is 2-5 business days; delivery time comes after that.
5. Printful ships and passes tracking back to Shopify. Enabled shipping notifications update the customer. Mixed orders can arrive in separate parcels.
6. You check exceptions and answer questions. Customers can use their order-status link or account, but can still email you for help.

Help: [Order notifications](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/orders/manage-orders/processing-orders) | [Production timing](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014007980-How-long-does-fulfillment-take) | [Tracking handoff](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014006880-How-does-Shopify-know-when-my-Printful-orders-are-shipped)

### What the status words mean

| You see | It means / what to do |
|---|---|
| Paid | Customer payment was collected. This does not mean it reached your bank yet. |
| Authorized / Pending | Payment is not necessarily collected. Investigate before releasing production. |
| Unfulfilled | Not yet recorded as shipped. Check Printful to see whether it is already being made. |
| Partially fulfilled | Some items shipped. Check the remaining items and parcel tracking. |
| Fulfilled | Shipment has been recorded; this does not prove delivery. |
| On hold / Failed | Read the problem in Printful and take action. Waiting alone may not fix it. |

### Two separate money movements

Customer pays the bakery through Shopify. Shopify Payments, once activated, pays out to the owner's bank on its payout schedule. Separately, Printful charges the bakery's billing method or Wallet. Keep funds available for production while a Shopify payout is pending.

Sales are not take-home profit: manufacturing, shipping, payment fees, refunds and monthly bills all reduce what remains. Review tax records with the accountant.

Help: [Printful payment flow](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014065439-How-do-I-accept-payments-for-my-orders) | [Shopify payouts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/payouts)

## Find an order and answer a customer

Start with the order record, then reply from the support inbox

### When a customer writes

1. Ask for their order number and the email used at checkout. Search Shopify > Orders. If they only have a checkout confirmation number, search that too.
2. Open the matching order. Check the purchased size/color, payment, shipping address, fulfillment and Timeline (the history of what happened).
3. For production or shipping questions, open the corresponding Printful order. Match the customer and items as well as the order reference.
4. Reply in the original email thread from shopsupport@sourdoughhousebakery.com. Give a specific next update date if the issue is not resolved.
5. Add a short private note in Shopify: problem, action taken, supplier ticket number and next follow-up date. A private note is not a message to the customer.

Help: [Finding and managing orders](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/managing-order-details) | [Confirmation numbers](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/setup/order-status-page/order-confirmation-numbers)

### Which messages happen automatically?

| Message | Your job |
|---|---|
| Order confirmation and shipping updates | Verify delivery during the launch test; usually no personal email is needed for a normal order. |
| Refund or cancellation notice | Keep customer notification selected when you perform the action; check the order history afterward. |
| Customer question or complaint | Read and reply yourself. An account page does not answer it for you. |
| Printful warning or billing failure | Read it, resolve the cause and confirm the order resumes. |

### Missing confirmation or login trouble

Check the email on the order and ask the customer to check spam. To resend a confirmation: Shopify > Orders > open order > Timeline > Resend email beneath that notification. Confirm the intended recipient before sending.

Customer accounts use an emailed six-digit sign-in code. Ask the customer to use their checkout email and the latest code; you do not create a password for them or ask them to send you the code. Their account address changes do not automatically correct an order already in production.

Guest buyers can follow the order-status link in their confirmation; Shopify may ask them to verify their details. If several customers stop receiving messages, contact Shopify Support. If your support mailbox cannot send or receive, contact the email administrator.

## A new order is not moving

Use this checklist before creating anything new

> Do not create another Printful order just because the Shopify order looks unfinished. First check whether Printful already has it. A duplicate can create another charge and another shipment.

### If the order is missing from Printful

1. Check Shopify payment status. Paid is different from Authorized or Pending. Do not mark an order paid just to force it through.
2. Check whether a Printful order already exists and whether Shopify shows a fulfillment request or an error. Import can take a few minutes.
3. If the tested handoff procedure is manual import: open the paid Shopify order, confirm the location is Printful, choose Request fulfillment, then Send fulfillment request.
4. If automatic import was configured and nothing appears after 10-15 minutes, use Printful > Stores > your store > Refresh data if available. If still missing, contact Printful with the Shopify order number and status screenshot.
5. If the item shows an incorrect location or a product-sync error, ask Printful Support to guide the correction. Do not disconnect the app, replace products or change product codes to experiment.

Help: [Manual fulfillment requests](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/6148086204316-How-do-I-manage-order-processing-and-fulfillment-in-Shopify) | [Missing imports](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014066019-Why-are-my-Shopify-orders-not-being-imported-to-Printful)

### If Printful has it but production has not started

| Problem shown | What you do |
|---|---|
| Needs confirmation / draft | Use the approval procedure established at handoff. Confirming can charge the bakery and start production. |
| Payment failed | Open the order and billing warning. Owner resolves the card/funds issue; retry only through the existing order, then verify its new status. |
| Address problem | Confirm the address with the buyer and use the available Printful address-edit action. Verify the saved destination. |
| Product unavailable / file issue | Ask Printful about the specific item. Tell the customer about any delay; obtain agreement before substituting a size, color or product. |
| Still being made | Use the order's current estimate. Contact Printful if overdue; do not promise a delivery date you cannot verify. |

### If Shopify flags possible fraud

Read the warning before authorizing production. If production is already underway, contact Printful promptly about the available options. Ask Shopify Support how to review the payment concern. A Paid status is not a guarantee against a later dispute.

Help: [Fraud warnings and disputes](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/chargebacks)

Close the loop: note the action, verify the order moved forward and update the customer if timing has changed.

## Changes, cancellations and refunds

Check Printful first when an order might already be in production

### Customer wants a cancellation or address change

1. Find the order in both systems immediately. Tell the customer you are checking whether the change is still possible.
2. In Printful, inspect the order. Change order or Change address appears only when the available change can still be made. A Shopify address edit alone is not proof Printful received it.
3. For a cancellation still allowed in Printful, use the order's three-dot menu > Cancel order. Verify the cancellation. Once production starts, a normal refundable cancellation is generally unavailable; contact Printful if unclear.
4. After production is confirmed stopped, open Shopify > Orders > the order > More actions > Cancel order. Review whether a refund already exists; choose the correct refund action, reason and customer notification.
5. Check both histories and send the customer the confirmed result. Canceling in Printful does not cancel or refund the customer's Shopify order.

Help: [Printful changes and cancellation](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014007060-Can-I-change-or-cancel-my-order-after-submitting-it) | [Shopify cancellation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/canceling-orders)

### Owner decides to refund a customer

1. Open the correct Shopify order. Review prior refunds, cancellation history and any active payment dispute to avoid paying twice. A refund will not necessarily stop production.
2. Choose Refund. Review the items/quantity, shipping refund if applicable, tax calculation, original payment method and final amount. For these made-to-order goods, do not restock an item that is not actually being returned to sellable stock.
3. Enter the reason and keep the customer notification enabled. Read the final amount and recipient carefully, then submit once. Shopify refunds cannot be undone.
4. Confirm the refund appears in the order history. Tell the customer it was issued; bank processing time varies. If it fails or the customer cannot see it after the stated processing time, contact Shopify Support.

Help: [Shopify refund steps](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/orders/refund-cancel-order)

> A Printful credit is money returned to the bakery, usually its Printful Wallet. It does not refund the customer. A customer refund must be handled separately in Shopify.

Help: [The two refund flows](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014065439-How-do-I-accept-payments-for-my-orders)

If production cannot be stopped, explain what is possible under the store policy. Do not promise a free remake or cancellation before checking. An owner-funded courtesy remedy is a separate business decision.

## Delivery problems and product complaints

You remain the customer's contact while Printful investigates

| Customer says | Your next action |
|---|---|
| Only part arrived | Compare items and tracking for each parcel. Explain any separate shipment. Report items genuinely missing from the expected parcel. |
| It is late | Check the current estimate, tracking and address. Ask Printful about a stalled order; keep the customer updated. |
| Delivered, but not here | Ask them to check the delivery area, household/front desk and local carrier. Printful does not normally fund replacements for parcels marked delivered. Investigate before choosing a remedy. |
| Returned to sender | Read Printful's notice promptly. Confirm the address and reason, then ask about reshipment and cost. Do not create another order until the original is accounted for. |
| Wrong size / changed mind | Compare the order with what arrived. Correct item but poor fit follows the no voluntary size/change-of-mind return policy. An incorrectly supplied size is a different issue: report it. |

Help: [Delivered but missing](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014007100-What-if-the-tracking-says-Delivered-but-my-customer-didn-t-get-the-package) | [Coverage and returned parcels](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014006840-What-is-Printful-s-return-and-refund-policy)

### Damaged, misprinted, defective or wrong item

1. Ask for the order number, a clear photo of the whole item, a close-up of the issue and the label/packaging where relevant. Ask the customer to keep the item while you review it.
2. In Printful > Orders, open the affected order and choose Report problem. Select the issue and preferred remedy, describe each affected item, attach photos and submit.
3. Save the ticket number and a follow-up date. If the button is missing, contact Printful Support instead of abandoning the claim.
4. Read Printful's decision. If a replacement is approved, confirm destination and tracking. If a supplier refund is approved, separately handle the customer's resolution in Shopify. Avoid ordering a second replacement while a claim is still open.

Help: [Submit a problem report](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014066699-How-do-I-report-a-problem-with-my-order)

### Put the supplier deadlines on your calendar

Damaged/defective/misprinted: claim within 30 days of receipt. Lost in transit: within 30 days of estimated delivery. Printful's report instructions use more than 5 business days past the estimated delivery date for non-arrival reports; ask support sooner if concerned. Returned parcels also need prompt action: Printful can donate unclaimed items after 30 days.

Help: [Claim deadlines](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014007180-How-long-do-I-have-to-submit-a-return-or-exchange-claim)

> These are supplier reimbursement rules, not the full extent of customer rights. Follow the published store policy and applicable law. Do not ask customers to mail merchandise to the bakery or Printful without confirmed instructions.

## Replies you can adapt and send

Replace every bracketed item; send only facts you have checked

### Acknowledge a new problem

> Hi [name], thanks for letting me know. I'm checking order [number] and will update you by [day/date], even if I'm still waiting for an answer. - [owner name], Sourdough House Bakery

### The item is still being made

> Hi [name], I checked your order and it is currently being made. The latest estimated shipping date is [verified estimate]. Delivery time comes after production, and you'll receive tracking when it ships. I'll check again on [date].

If there is no reliable estimate, say: "I've asked our production partner for an updated estimate and will follow up by [date]."

### Separate packages

> Hi [name], your order is shipping in [number] packages. [Item] is [status], and [other item] is [status]. Here is the available tracking: [link]. I'll check the remaining item on [date].

### Ask for photos of a problem

> Hi [name], I'm sorry there's a problem with your item. Please send your order number, a photo of the whole item and a close-up of the issue. If the package was damaged or the size was wrong, please include the packaging or size label too. Please keep the item for now; I'll explain the next step after reviewing it.

### Cancellation request received

> Hi [name], I'm checking whether production has started. Since each item is made to order, I can't confirm cancellation yet. I'll let you know the result as soon as I have it.

### Correct item, but customer wants another size

> Hi [name], I'm sorry the fit wasn't what you expected. Our merchandise is made to order, so our policy does not offer returns or exchanges for a size selected at checkout or a change of mind. If the size received differs from your order, or there is a defect, please send a photo so I can help.

### Refund already verified in Shopify

> Hi [name], I've issued a refund of [amount and currency] for order [number] to your original payment method. You should receive a confirmation. The time it takes to appear depends on your bank or payment provider.

Keep a reply in the same email thread. Check unresolved conversations again on the date you promised. Acknowledging a message does not mean the problem is resolved.
