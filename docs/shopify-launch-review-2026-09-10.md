## 2. Pricing
The saved setup records describe 30% markup with half-dollar rounding. This fits examples such as $13.05 beanie cost → $17 retail and $24.95 apron cost → $32.50 retail.

A 30% markup means price = cost × 1.30. It produces a 23.1% gross product margin, before payment fees, shipping subsidy, discounts, refunds, advertising, monthly platform costs and tax costs. A 30% gross margin needs price = cost ÷ 0.70; a 40% gross margin needs cost ÷ 0.60.

Printful itself suggests adding at least 30% of base cost while considering shipping and non-Printful expenses. Its wording calls this a margin, but mathematically a percentage added to cost is markup. It is a starting rule, not a guarantee of net profit. [Printful pricing guide](https://www.printful.com/payments-guide)

### Actual comparison stores
These are observed merchandise prices, not evidence of competitors’ supplier costs, profitability, or sales conversion.
- Kitchen in the Shire: logo tee $34, apron $38, tote $37, water bottle $36, 40 oz travel mug $34, mugs $14 and $24.98, hoodie $79. Mostly whole-dollar prices. [Store](https://kitchen-in-the-shire-shop.fourthwall.com/)
- Pitchoun Bakery: tees $10–12, mug $12, tote $10, apron $25. Different garments and sourcing economics are unknown; its low tee prices cannot be assumed viable for this Printful catalog. [Store](https://pitchounbakery.com/shop/merchandising-items/)

For this bakery, use whole-dollar prices and consistent category tiers. Keep logo colors the same price. A $3 large-size increment is easy to understand where costs require it; existing apparel’s upper tier is 2XL. Avoid automatic free shipping or blanket discounts until mixed-cart economics are tested.

Aim provisionally for 30–40% product gross margin on fully decorated manufacturing cost, then require adequate dollar contribution after all variable costs. Cheap items generally need a higher percentage margin to cover fixed payment fees and support. This is a recommended planning target, not a market-wide norm.

### Why low-priced items need attention
Live Printful catalog base prices: Classic White Mug $6.07–$9.69, accent mug $8.11–$9.13, latte mug $8.46, magnet $3.39–$5.99, beanie $13.05, apron $24.95. Catalog prices exclude shipping, applicable tax, extra placements and other charges; final destination/account pricing can differ.

At $8 retail, the 11 oz mug leaves $1.93 before processing. Using the publicly listed Basic standard-card rate of 2.9% + $0.30, that falls to about $1.40 before shipping, taxes, overhead or support. At $14, the same simplified calculation leaves about $7.22. Fees can also apply to shipping and taxes collected; premium/international cards or other methods differ. [Shopify rates](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)

Use an order-level calculation:
Customer merchandise revenue after discounts + shipping collected − decorated Printful product cost − Printful shipping − unrecovered supplier tax − payment/platform transaction fees − expected replacement/refund/support cost.
Customer sales tax collected is a liability to remit, not profit. Allocate monthly Shopify and app charges separately to see business profit.

Do not buy a supplier subscription solely for advertised discounts. Compare actual monthly savings on this catalog against the subscription fee.

## 3. Domain and launch sequence
Keep the bakery site on Vercel and baked-goods orders in Hotplate.

| Purpose | Address |
|---|---|
| Bakery website | sourdoughhousebakery.com / www |
| Shopify merchandise | shop.sourdoughhousebakery.com |
| Optional branded customer accounts | account.sourdoughhousebakery.com |

For the storefront, add this record in the current DNS manager:
- Type: CNAME
- Host/name: shop
- Target: shops.myshopify.com
- TTL: default or 1 hour

This subdomain connection does not require moving the root domain or changing its A record. Preserve root/www, email MX, and existing mail-authentication records. [Shopify subdomain instructions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/domains/add-a-domain/connecting-domains/connect-subdomain)

Then:
1. Shopify Settings → Domains → Connect existing domain → enter shop.sourdoughhousebakery.com.
2. Verify the CNAME; add Shopify’s exact TXT verification record only if requested.
3. Wait for connection and TLS/HTTPS readiness, then make the shop subdomain the primary online-store domain.
4. Configure the optional account subdomain through Customer accounts and use the exact destination Shopify supplies. Do not reuse the storefront target by assumption.
5. Authenticate the sender email using Shopify-provided DNS records, preserving other email senders.
6. Publish the approved branded theme when operational checks pass.
7. Add/deploy bakery header/footer Shop links to the verified subdomain. Keep Hotplate Order links.
8. Remove storefront password protection when ready to accept public orders.
9. Test bakery → shop → product → cart → checkout → account/order tracking → bakery on phone and desktop; confirm final-domain links in emails.

DNS connection, theme publication and removing the password are separate steps. Connecting the hostname alone does not activate checkout or publish the draft.

## 4. Payments and ownership
For this US store, the normal path is Shopify Payments. Her existing standalone Stripe account is not the account this store ordinarily connects to. Shopify says standalone Stripe is unavailable to activate in regions where Shopify Payments is supported; Shopify manages payment reporting and operations in its admin. She can keep her existing Stripe account for its existing uses. [Gateway availability](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/third-party-providers/payment-gateway-availability)

The business owner completes Settings → Payments → Complete setup using the correct business identity, representative, address/tax details, verification documents if requested, and eligible payout bank account. Shopify Payments deposits net payouts to that bank; order payment and bank arrival are different events. Set up two-step authentication and inspect the actual payout schedule and any hold. [Account setup](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/onboarding/account-setup), [Payouts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/payouts)

There are two separate money flows:
- Customer → Shopify payment provider → bakery bank.
- Bakery’s Printful Wallet/billing method → Printful for manufacturing, shipping and supplier tax.

Printful does not collect the customer’s payment or wait for Shopify’s bank payout. Keep sufficient available funds/credit for fulfillment and refunds while payouts are pending. [Printful payment flow](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014065439-How-do-I-accept-payments-for-my-orders)

Shopify plan fees, card processing, supplier charges, and optional app subscriptions are separate costs. Basic is publicly listed at $39 monthly or $29/month billed yearly, with standard online card rates from 2.9% + $0.30; actual onboarding offers/rates must be checked. Basic has no additional staff accounts, so plan ownership and helper access properly if both siblings need admin access. [Plans](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)

## 5. Full operating process
### Customer creation and management
A visitor browses without an account. At checkout they supply contact, delivery and payment details; Shopify associates the order with a customer profile. Your current configuration allows guest checkout. Optional customer accounts use an emailed one-time six-digit code; customers can view orders and manage addresses/profile information. New sign-ins can create profiles. A Printful account is never required for the customer. [Shopify accounts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/customers/customer-accounts)

Your sister works primarily in Shopify Customers and Orders: look up a customer, inspect order history, add useful support notes/tags, and keep marketing consent distinct from transactional contact. Buying merchandise does not automatically authorize promotional messages. Bakery/Hotplate customer histories are separate unless a deliberate integration is introduced.

### Checkout and order acceptance
Shopify calculates merchandise, configured shipping, discounts and applicable tax. The provider authorizes/captures payment according to the chosen capture setting. Shopify creates the order and sends enabled order confirmation and staff notifications.

Keep payment status, fulfillment status and delivery status separate. Paid means payment succeeded; fulfilled generally means shipped; neither guarantees the parcel has been delivered.

### Order review and Printful handoff
Review the first orders for payment status, fraud warnings, address, selected variants, decorated costs, shipping and margin. Decide who checks the queue each business day.

Shopify currently uses manual fulfillment. Under that workflow the owner opens the paid order and requests fulfillment from Printful. However, Printful can also be configured to import synced orders independently, so both applications’ switches must be checked before treating manual Shopify settings as a production hold. Printful’s automatic confirmation/billing setting is separate from importing. [Order processing](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/6148086204316-How-do-I-manage-order-processing-and-fulfillment-in-Shopify), [Import and confirmation](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014066019-Why-are-my-Shopify-orders-not-being-imported-to-Printful)

Once validated, routine paid orders can be imported and confirmed automatically. Inspect exceptions daily; automation does not resolve failed billing, fraud, unavailable blanks, address issues or print-file holds.

### Manufacturing and shipment
Printful charges the merchant and produces the selected mapped item. The bakery usually does not print labels, pack goods or ship these merchandise orders. Production generally averages 2–5 business days, with shipping added; use product/destination estimates and disclose they can change. A shipping speed does not eliminate production time. [Timing](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014007980-How-long-does-fulfillment-take)

Printful sends fulfillment and tracking to Shopify when it ships. Mixed products can ship separately; Shopify receives tracking for each shipment. Leave these fulfillment updates to Printful rather than manually marking items shipped. [Tracking integration](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014006880-How-does-Shopify-know-when-my-Printful-orders-are-shipped)

### Emails and customer communication
Configure and test:
- Customer: order confirmation, shipping/tracking, cancellation and refund notices; account login codes.
- Owner: new-order alerts, Printful billing/hold/fulfillment exceptions, customer inquiries.
- Optional later: abandoned checkout, review requests and marketing campaigns under the relevant consent/settings.

Use a monitored domain sender/reply-to mailbox; a configured address is not proof that anyone receives replies. Authenticate it in Shopify Notifications. Shopify supplies DKIM/SPF-related CNAME records and requires an appropriate DMARC setup for branded sending. Test inbox and spam placement, branding, policy links and destination URLs. [Sender email](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/initial-setup/setup-your-email)

### Questions, delivery problems and returns
The bakery owns the customer relationship. Publish one support route and a realistic response target, such as within two business days. Customer → bakery; bakery → Printful when a supplier claim is needed.

| Situation | Owner action |
|---|---|
| Sizing/material/care question | Answer from verified product specifications and size guide before purchase. |
| Cancellation/address correction | Check production immediately. Request cancellation/change and verify acceptance in both systems before promising it. |
| Payment taken but no production | Inspect paid/captured status, fulfillment request/location, sync, Printful billing and holds. Avoid creating duplicate orders. |
| Wrong, damaged, defective or misprinted item | Collect order number and clear photos; submit Printful claim; communicate replacement/refund resolution. |
| Wrong size/color chosen or change of mind | Apply the bakery’s published policy. Printful does not fund these returns/exchanges; a courtesy replacement costs the bakery. |
| Lost in transit | Check tracking/address and expected delivery; escalate within Printful’s claim deadline. |
| Tracking says delivered but missing | Investigate delivery details/carrier; do not promise automatic Printful reimbursement. |
| Returned to sender | Confirm address and reason; arrange reshipment and explain any charge under the published policy. |
| Chargeback | Respond through the payment provider with order, correspondence and tracking evidence; monitor its deadline. |

Printful generally covers approved damaged/defective/misprinted items with reshipment or refund; no physical return is normally needed. Its policy excludes wrong size/color and buyer’s remorse. Your customer policy and applicable consumer law govern your obligations; the supplier policy alone does not settle them. [Returns](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014006840-What-is-Printful-s-return-and-refund-policy)

Claims: defects within 30 days of receipt; lost shipments within 30 days of estimated delivery. A returned package must be claimed within 30 days of Printful’s notification. [Claim deadlines](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014007180-How-long-do-I-have-to-submit-a-return-or-exchange-claim), [Returned packages](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014066499-My-order-is-being-returned-to-sender-what-should-I-do)

A Printful refund goes to the merchant, normally its Printful Wallet. It does not automatically refund the Shopify customer: issue and verify the customer refund separately. Likewise, a customer refund does not guarantee manufacturing has stopped. A Shopify cancellation request can reach Printful before fulfillment starts, but can be rejected after production begins. [Refund flow](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014065439-How-do-I-accept-payments-for-my-orders), [Cancellation behavior](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/6148086204316-How-do-I-manage-order-processing-and-fulfillment-in-Shopify)

### Reconciliation and ongoing management
Daily: paid/unfulfilled orders, billing/production holds, late shipments and customer messages.
Weekly: Shopify payouts versus orders/refunds/fees, Printful charges, actual profit, inventory exceptions.
Monthly: subscriptions, margins, replacement rate, support workload and sales tax records.

Determine tax registrations and product taxability with the owner’s accountant, configure Shopify accordingly, and handle Printful resale exemptions where valid. Shopify calculates tax but does not automatically file/remit it unless a supported filing service is specifically set up. [Shopify tax responsibility](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/taxes/)
